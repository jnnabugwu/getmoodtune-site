Delete your account

Last updated October 17, 2026

You can ask us to delete your MoodTune account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

Email privacy@getmoodtune.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my MoodTune account". We'll confirm by email and delete your account within 30 days. Usually it's much sooner.

In-app deletion is coming in a future update. When it arrives, this page will explain where to find it.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your email address and sign-in details.
  • Your saved History: song titles, artists, and moods.
  • Your Spotify connection and its access tokens, if you connected Spotify.

What we keep

  • Mood accuracy records: the mood we showed you, your "does this feel right?" answer, and your device model and app version. Once your account is gone, these records aren't linked to you, and they never contain titles, artists, file names, or audio.
  • Crash reports our error-reporting provider already holds. These expire under that provider's retention policy.

We never store your audio, so there's none to delete.

Used MoodTune without an account?

Then we hold no account data about you. The mood accuracy records from guest use aren't linked to any person, so we can't find or delete them individually.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
